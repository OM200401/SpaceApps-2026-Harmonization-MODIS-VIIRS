One Burning Calendar

Two NASA satellites, one trustworthy fire record. MODIS (1 km, 2000+) and VIIRS (375 m, 2012+) count fires differently — VIIRS sees many more small fires, so the raw record fakes a jump in 2012. We calibrated the sensors on their 2012–2023 overlap, by fire intensity and season, and fused them into a single harmonized calendar. VIIRS is the reference; MODIS-era harmonized counts estimate what VIIRS would have seen.
